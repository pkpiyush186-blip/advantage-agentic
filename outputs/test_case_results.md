## Two cases needed new capability

TC08 and TC15 described behaviour the prototype did not have. Both were built rather than having their checks softened:

- **TC08 (pacing).** A channel that cannot spend what it was given now raises an `under_delivery` flag, and the guardrail caps it at roughly what it actually delivered instead of freezing it - it is not misbehaving, it simply cannot absorb the budget. Thresholds live in the rulebook (`under_delivery_pct`, `pacing_headroom_pct`).
- **TC15 (budget cut).** The weekly total now lives on the case record and can be set per week, so finance changing the budget mid-season is a data change. The agent still may not change the total itself - the rulebook forbids it.
